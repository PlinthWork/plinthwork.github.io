One foundation. Every layer connected.

Plinthwork builds clean, self-contained C++ software from the ground up—where every tool rests directly on the layer beneath it.

The Name & Idea

Why is it called Plinthwork?

In architecture, a plinth is the heavy, solid base block that supports an entire structure built on top of it. Our software works the exact same way: nothing floats on heavy external frameworks. Plinth2D forms the pure CPU rendering base at the bottom, PlinthUI sits directly on top of it to build interfaces, and PlinthDE brings everything together into a fast Linux desktop environment.
